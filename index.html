Briefing · ~5 min

The Assignment

You're building a scavenger hunt as an anniversary gift. The best hunts aren't about clever puzzles — they're a guided tour of your story, with a payoff at the end.

Over 4 short modules you'll fill out a build form, one rule at a time. At the end the page turns your answers into a secret organizer's plan and printable clue cards.

🤫 It's a surprise — don't leave this page open where your partner can see it. Your answers aren't saved if you close the tab, so print or copy your plan at the end.

Module 1 · ~10 min

💌 The Story

  • Rule 1 — Start with why. The hunt should say one thing: "I remember us." Everything else serves that.
  • Rule 2 — Give it a hook. Hand over a first envelope with a short, intriguing opening message so they know the game has started.
2–3 sentences that explain it's a hunt, without giving anything away.
Module 2 · ~10 min

📍 The Stops

  • Rule 3 — Meaning beats fancy. First date spot, the bench where you talked for hours — real memories beat expensive locations.
  • Rule 4 — Keep it short and close. 3–6 stops you can walk or drive between in your time cap. A hunt that drags is a chore.
  • Rule 5 — Safe and allowed. Public, open, daylight-friendly places. Never hide things where you'd need to trespass or disturb a business.
Module 3 · ~15 min

🧩 The Clues

  • Rule 6 — Signpost everything. Make it obvious where to look and what counts as a clue — an envelope with their name on it, never a loose scrap they might toss.
  • Rule 7 — Mix it up and ramp up. Vary the clue types and start easy so the first win comes fast.
  • Rule 8 — Every clue gets a hint. A stuck partner stops having fun. Print the hint small at the bottom.
Module 4 · ~10 min

🎁 The Finale

  • Rule 9 — End on feeling, not stuff. The payoff is you, plus a message that lands. A gift is a bonus.
  • Rule 10 — Test it and have a backup. Have a friend walk the route, and plan for rain.
